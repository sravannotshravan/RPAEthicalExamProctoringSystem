Non-Invasive Visual Monitoring Policy

This examination system performs limited real-time visual environment checks using edge computer vision. We prioritize candidate privacy and transparency.

What This POC Detects:
  • Presence of a single candidate face in frame
  • Temporary absence of face (out of frame)
  • Presence of multiple faces in webcam frame
What This POC NEVER Does:
  • Does NOT identify individuals or verify facial identity
  • Does NOT store continuous webcam footage or biometric templates
  • Does NOT store facial biometric embeddings
  • Does NOT automatically classify candidates as "cheating"
Human-in-the-Loop: Any visual anomalies detected by OpenCV are logged as neutral events for faculty review only.
Examination Instructions:
  • You will have {{ exam.duration_minutes }} minutes to complete all questions once started.
  • Your webcam will be accessed to check visual presence during the test.
  • You can run test cases and submit each question independently.
  • Ensure you are in a reasonably lit room with your face visible in the preview window.
